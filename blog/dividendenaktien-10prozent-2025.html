🇬🇧 English Summary

High Dividend Stocks 10%+ – Shipping, BDCs & Hard Assets

This analysis covers dividend stocks paying 10%+ annual yields, focusing on hard asset sectors. Covered stocks include tanker companies (TORM, Frontline, Nordic American Tankers), BDCs (Business Development Companies with 8–12% yields), and upstream oil producers with variable high dividends.

Key insight: 10%+ dividends in shipping are variable (paid from Free Cashflow, not fixed). In strong freight rate markets (high TCE), tanker stocks can yield 15–20%+. In weak markets, dividends drop proportionally. BDC dividends are more stable but depend on credit quality and interest rate environment.
